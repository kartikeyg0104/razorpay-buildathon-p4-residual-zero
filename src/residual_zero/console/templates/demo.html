What a judge should understand from this product

What is this product?
An AI-powered settlement reconciliation controller. The deterministic engine proves residual and uniqueness. AI investigates. Humans decide genuine ambiguity. The system never auto-clears without proof.
Why is 1:1 insufficient?
Exact 1:1 matching finds 0 out of 239. A payout is a signed net of many lines, not one invoice.
What makes this different from fuzzy matching?
Fuzzy matching picks a likely pair. This system enumerates exact paise subsets and refuses when more than one explanation fits.
What proves a match?
Residual 0 and UNIQUE on a FULL pool and the ordering gate. Residual 0 alone is not CLEARED.
Two valid explanations?
AMBIGUOUS. Proof Explorer shows A/B. The engine will not pick a winner.
What does AI actually do?
Select read-only tools, retrieve evidence, compare sources and equations, explain, prioritize. It does not calculate financial truth.
Can AI clear a transaction?
No. REFUSE_CLEAR. writes_cleared stays false.
Why is auto-clear zero?
Official unique is 0. Auto-clear follows UNIQUE, not residual-zero.
Missing evidence?
NOT_RECONCILED / NONE_FOUND. The controller names the gap. It does not invent members.
Can a human intervene?
Yes. Human review is a separate event from AI investigation. Status cannot be CLEARED here.
Mathematical proof?
Credit page + Proof Explorer. Integer paise. Candidate equations displayed, not chosen.
Audit the AI?
/audit and artifacts/console/ai_audit.jsonl. No API keys. Claim validation is recorded.
Technical details

The live NVIDIA NIM rewrite is optional. If the provider fails or the key is rejected, fallback templates still run. That is not a live provider pass.
